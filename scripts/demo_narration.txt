Turnproof belongs to the same story as all of Armada's prize work: use new
intelligence to expand what people can build without surrendering the ground
beneath them. Imagination opens possibility. Evidence makes it durable.

Conversational software can sound confident while its state is wrong. Turnproof
protects the boundary where Alexa Plus interpretation becomes durable meaning
or an application effect.

We begin with partial information. Missing facts stay missing; no defaults are
invented. The user answers no, creating a revision and semantic digest.

Then the answer is corrected to yes. Active meaning changes, while the
superseded value remains visible in the ledger.

An action is proposed and bound to that exact state. New evidence advances the
conversation. The old approval no longer matches, so Turnproof returns State
Changed and produces no effect.

The contracts themselves generate attacks for unknowns, corrections, retries,
concurrency, stale actions, and order convergence. All sixteen pass.

Then we switch from diagnosis to a structurally different handoff contract. The
same reducer, firewall, ledger, and receipts remain.

This is Armada Ventures' modus operandi: use LLMs to augment judgment and help
design, without making critical trust depend on them. In an uncertain era of
LLM reliance, deterministic contracts and reproducible evidence provide safe
harbor. Imagination in the interface. Discipline underneath. That is the story.
